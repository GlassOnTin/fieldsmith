## Coupon and law conventions

Coupon: N³ unit cells of side c = 2 mm, gyroid sheet lattice, meshed by isosurface stuffing at h = t (one mesh cell per wall; t from the density calibration at the nominal ρ), 1000 MPa coupon material, ν = 0.3. Free-slip platens: traction −σ·nₓ on the planar material patches of the x = L face (σ = 10 MPa), ux = 0 on the x = 0 patches, two point constraints remove the residual rigid modes. Apparent modulus E* = Σ / ε with Σ the macroscopic section stress and ε the volume-weighted least-squares slope of ux over the interior band (1 cell from each platen). The law: E*/E = c·ρ^n with c = 616.9 MPa, n = 1.426, least-squares fitted in log-log space on ρ_mesh/E_int pairs; V6 compares the law at the COUPON'S MEASURED density (ρ_mesh) with the measured E*. 1000 MPa scaling matches the coupon material. The equilibrium identities (∫σxx dV = Σ Fₓ·x and ⟨σxx⟩ = −Σ/ρL² over the material) gate each run at ≤ 0.5 %: a load-patch assembly regression shows there first.
