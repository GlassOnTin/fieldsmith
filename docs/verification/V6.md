## Coupon and law conventions

Coupon: N³ unit cells of side c = 2 mm, gyroid sheet lattice, meshed by isosurface stuffing at h = t (one mesh cell per wall; t from the density calibration at the nominal ρ), 1000 MPa coupon material, ν = 0.3. Free-slip platens: traction −σ·nₓ on the planar material patches of the x = L face (σ = 10 MPa), ux = 0 on the x = 0 patches, two point constraints remove the residual rigid modes. Apparent modulus E* = Σ / ε with Σ the macroscopic section stress and ε the volume-weighted least-squares slope of ux over the interior band (1 cell from each platen). The law: E*/E = c·ρ^n with c = 616.9 MPa, n = 1.426, least-squares fitted in log-log space on ρ_mesh/E_int pairs; V6 compares the law at the COUPON'S MEASURED density (ρ_mesh) with the measured E*. 1000 MPa scaling matches the coupon material. The fit inputs were re-measured (2026-10-15) after the max-face junction scan reshaped the mesh at the coupon's box planes; the law constants were not re-fitted. The equilibrium identities (∫σxx dV = Σ Fₓ·x and ⟨σxx⟩ = −Σ/ρL² over the material) gate each run at ≤ 0.5 %: a load-patch assembly regression shows there first.

## Coverage and limits

This case covers gyroid sheet mode only (GOAL V6). The other five law rows (primitive and diamond in sheet mode, and all three families in network mode) share the same engine mechanism and harness, and their sweeps are in `crates/fieldsmith-verify/examples/tpms-coupon.rs` history, but they are not re-checked here as automated cases: their coupon setups needed per-family shift/band choices, which V6's single fixed configuration cannot carry. Law support is the measured band ρ ∈ [0.18, 0.40]; outside it the engine clamps (documented in `fill.rs`).
