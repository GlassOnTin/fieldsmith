## What the first-pair fit gets wrong

The two-point fit is exact over its own points and still wrong beyond them: it projects the 750k-DOF rung at 6519 ms (referee) / 14790 ms (N100) against measured 9793 / 25573 — under by 33 % / 42 %. The first pair sits on the cheap side of the curve; extrapolating it far is the estimator's least reliable act. That is why (a) every projection travels with its residual, and a two-point fit reports "residual unmeasured" rather than a fabricated 0 %; and (b) when a rung has fewer than two local points, the CLI's budget/tolerance projections fall back to `seed_median` — the median wall of same machine-class rows — a level, never a claim about slope (`crates/fieldsmith-cli/src/main.rs`).
