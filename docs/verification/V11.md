## Method

The STL bar goes through the real import chain (read → weld → segment → voxelize → hydrate → document node), then `fieldsmith_core::analyse` at h = 2.0, 1.0, 0.5 mm (field → isosurface-stuffed tets → P2 solve → nodal stress recovery). The tip value is the probed z-displacement at the centre of the loaded face; the stress value is the probed σxx at the top fibre at mid-span. The cap/tip regions are matched through `region_facets` (the same path the app uses), and their claimed area is asserted against the face's 100 mm² at every mesh size.
