## Coverage and limits

One load case and one family/mode (gyroid sheet) are covered here; the other laws are the same engine code path, and their coupon measurements sit in the V6 harness history. The engine makes a single grading pass — densities are not re-iterated from the graded stress — so this verifies what one pass achieves, as the fill module documents. The element-modulus mapping is measured at this case's element size: a probe on the engine's own mesh and boundary conditions read it at −2.1 % (h = 1.0 mm) and −0.5 % (h = 0.75 mm) against the resolved reference below (`examples/v7_mf_probe.rs`). The blend from Voigt mixing to the homogenised law over h/cell ∈ [1, 1.5] is an interpolation between the two measured regimes, not itself a measurement; at 1.0 mm the blend weight is zero, so this run sits fully in the Voigt regime. The resolved walls at h ≈ 0.29–0.33 sit at the law's own measurement discretisation (V6 coupons at h = t, one P2 element per wall): the thinnest ρmin walls carry about one element across, the thickest (ρmax) about two — the resolved mesh study bounds the sensitivity this leaves in the reference. The mass check is a soft cross-check between two discretised integrations of the same field, not a physical measurement. Designs whose achieved lattice density falls outside the measured band [0.18, 0.40] (thin sheet walls clamped to the authored minimum printable wall) are governed by the clamp behaviour documented in `fill.rs`, not by this case.
