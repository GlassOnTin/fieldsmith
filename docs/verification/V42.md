## The fit recipe's [110] inversion

V36 printed its S44 recipe as S44 = 2(1/E45 − S12), which is not the cubic [110] inversion: the [110] modulus is 1/E[110] = S11 − (S11 − S12 − S44/2)/2, i.e. S44 = 4/E45 − 2(S11 + S12). The two agree only when E45 = E0. With the corrected inversion the fitted tensors reproduce V36's measured ratios exactly (the check tolerances are the fit constants' 5-digit rounding); the C11 and C12/C11 side of the fit is unaffected — it comes from E[100] and ν, not from E[110].
