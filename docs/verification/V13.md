## Case

A 100 × 10 × 10 mm steel box clamped at one end, no loads. The modal run meshes the document as the static path does (the supports only) and solves the first four natural frequencies on P2 tetrahedra with a consistent mass matrix. The measured values are rung means of each degenerate transverse pair; the pair at ranks 1–2 is the first bending mode in the two transverse directions, ranks 3–4 the second.

## Reference

Euler–Bernoulli cantilever, computed from the document's parameters: f_n = (βnL)² √(EI/(ρAL⁴)) / 2π with β₁L = 1.8751, β₂L = 4.6941, I = bh³/12 = 833.3 mm⁴. f1 = 815.4 Hz, f2 = 5109.9 Hz. The pair-2 gap is expected: Euler–Bernoulli neglects shear deformation and rotary inertia; at L/h = 10 the Timoshenko effects lower the second mode by about 4–5 %, and the FE answer converges to the Timoshenko value, not the Euler–Bernoulli one.

## Solver

The eigen route is inverse power iteration with deflation (fenics-rs), its inner solve the static path's two-level PCG, built once and reused across the run. The Jacobi-only inner CG the route previously used flatlined at the fine rung (45,123 DOF: relative residual 1.1 of 1e-9 after the 1000-iteration budget, measured before the change); the static path's preconditioner converges the same system to 1e-9 in about 48 iterations at this rung (V15's measurement). A second fix stopped the power iteration a percent or two early: its stopping criterion compared the Rayleigh quotient against a fixed anchor, and the mass rescale inside the route shrinks the quotient to a fraction of the frequency, so the fixed anchor was an absolute threshold in disguise; the criterion now compares consecutive quotients to each other.
