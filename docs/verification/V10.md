## Region-driven extension (the milestone ask)

```
fieldsmith 1
name "perforated-grid-solve"
material petg
mesh mesh1 src="Meshy_AI_...stl" units=mm up=z vox=0.25 hash="a904b05ff8ce3f09" ...
region bottom = mesh1.bottom
region top = mesh1.top
region ym = mesh1.ny
extend ext1 bottom dir=(0, 0, -1) len=10
support clamp ym
load press top force=(0, 0, -100)
```

`extend` evaluates as a swept solid: the region's mesh leaf swept along `dir × len`,
occupancy mask, signed EDT (chamfer). The union body is `mesh1 ∪ ext1`.

## Solving the imported part

Probe used during the fix (examples/v10_solve_probe.rs — deleted after measurement; see
the clippy-gate note below) ran `core::analyse` and independently counted facets:
| doc | boundary facets | `bottom` claims | `top` claims | verdict |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| original (58,366-tri body) | 9,044 | 1,981 | 1,978 | OK |
| extended (140,866-tri body) | 21,562 | **0** | 1,929 | "does not touch surface" |

This is correct behaviour, and it is the interesting finding of the section: the extended
solid **consumes the labelled band**. The sweep interiorises the plate's −z surface at
z ≈ 0 — the union's boundary there is gone by construction ("without affecting the
existing mesh" is not "the mesh surface still exists under the skirt"). On the extended
doc the plate-bottom `bottom` region therefore legitimately claims 0 facets and
`fixed_mask` refuses the support with "does not touch the part's surface";
`mesh1.bottom` on the *un-extended* part claims 1,981 facets (worst leaf-corner residual
3.9e-11 against the placed shape's band-exact tolerance 1e-6·h).

The swept solid has no face selector of its own (a Face on an Extend node falls through
to the primitive face branch — a gap: see Known gaps).

**Solve rows** (CLI `fieldsmith solve --h 0.5 --report`; `/tmp/mesh-import/{solve-orig,solve}.fsm`;
`/usr/bin/time -v`):

```

            Mesh 21 ms, solve 1264 ms, 28 iterations. | Load 1 | 2.29 MPa | 0.0034 mm |
```

Both rows solve; the extended body is ~3.7× stiffer in the direction measured, as the
10 mm skirt would have it. The primary row is the un-extended part under a bottom clamp
(physically the sensible fixture); the secondary row's clamp had to move to a surviving
wall — the consumed-band analysis above. This is the first mesh-leaf solve in the repo;
a dedicated verification case (load application over patch-labeled boundary facets, mesh
convergence against a reference) is *not* in scope of this milestone as set, and is
listed as a gap.

**Dirty-data caveat, measured.** The GLB fixture used by the readers tests shipped with
3 of 6 quads wound inward (z−, x+, y+). The voxeliser voxelised the lying mesh honestly —
sign flood from the wound normals — yielding 586.8 mm³ for a 1000 mm³ cube before the
winding was corrected. On a flipped *mixed*/consistent-winding source the field is
inverted locally: a hand cube with the bottom face wound inward reads
`[3, 2, −0.1] → −0.1` (solid) and `[3, 2, 6.1] → +0.1` (air) — mirrored across z = 3.
Today's shipped fixtures and the meshy file are 0-open-edge consistent windings only;
imports of mixed-winding meshes are not diagnosed. Segment (`weld::segment`) *is*
winding-insensitive (absolute dihedral normals), so patches still label on dirty meshes;
the SDF does not follow.

**Band-exactness probes** (sphere fixture, voxel 0.25): centre clamp to the conservative
band at −0.75 (3 cells of 0.25); pole at 0.5 below the surface measured −0.4998 —
1.5e-4 error, the band contract. The GLB fixture's 586.8 → 1000 mm³ fix and the sphere
assertion sign fix were both *test-side* corrections in the same review — the engine
numbers measured true both times.

## Known gaps

- Region thresholds (corner gate, 0.25h centroid gate, 0.7 gradient/normal cones) are
  tuned empirically on this file and the fixtures; not proven over the whole voxel/h
  plane. Rule of thumb in the dialog: voxel ≤ solve h / 2, stated, not enforced.
- The meshy GLB family is unproven as a family — the milestone names GLB/OBJ/STL/3MF;
  *3MF import* is deferred (it is now also our export format, so it reads elsewhere):
  only GLB and STL exercised with synthetic fixtures; OBJ with one.
- `Filled` (graded lattice) on mesh-node docs: untested in this milestone (bounds/eval
  paths are shape-agnostic; likely composes, not measured).
- The swept solid exposes no face selector of its own — a Face on an Extend node falls
  to the primitive branch, so the skirt's side walls cannot be labelled today.
- Swept side walls are voxel-resolution features: chamfer EDT smoothes them over ~1–2
  cells at 0.1–0.25 mm voxels; a perforated patch's holes come out jagged at voxel scale.
- No band certificate: the sweep mask's band-edge gradient lip (seen at `[0,0,−17]`) is
  a stated observation, not a proof.
- Support on the extended doc must be re-placed on surviving geometry; the tool states
  "does not touch" rather than explaining the band consumption.
- `--info bbox` is centred; z matches within rounding (0.0001 of our census).
- Mixed-winding input meshes voxelise honestly but produce an inverted/jumbled field;
  no diagnostic is raised (winding-insensitive patch labelling still works).
