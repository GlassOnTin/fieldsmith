## Not here

- A per-point tolerance: one `tolerance` field applies to every point (defaulting like the ladder's); there is no grammar to give point k a different band than point k+1.
- Params outside the address grammar `<id>.<attr>[.<k>]`: the sweep addresses a named numeric attribute on the parsed document — region selectors, solve settings, and extruded-profile vertices are not names in that grammar, and the sweep cannot add or remove nodes.
- Any warm-start sharing between points: each point clones the document, re-meshes at its value, and bills its own rung walls; nothing is carried across points.
- A per-point budget: the points share the declared schedule or tolerance; an outcome gate evaluated per value is the lens's job, not the grammar's.
