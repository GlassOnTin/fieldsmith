## Method

The document `examples/bracket-filled.fsm` (name "Bracket", material `petg`) is built through the same `graded()` engine the Make lens calls, then handed to `fieldsmith-mesh::export::surface` at h = 0.25 mm. The volume reference is a field integral of the SAME graded field at h = 0.094 mm — the probe (`v8_probe.rs`, release) measured its fine-reference ladder at 23484.2 ± 12.6 mm³ there (0.05 %), so the field side of the comparison is fixed independently before the mesh is built. Defects come from `TriMesh::defects` (edge-map scan); self-intersections from the spatial-hash scan. The STL is written and read back; the 3MF is unpacked (central directory + raw-deflate inflate) and its model XML checked.

## Non-manifold edges of the graded fill

2276 edges with ≥ 3 incident triangles at h = 0.25. The classification probe (`v8_probe2.rs`) walks 26 directions per endpoint through the field: 43 of 3403 endpoints carry vanishing material (true pinch points of the fill surface, thickness → 0); every other endpoint keeps healthy interior either side (median span ≥ 1.2 mm, p10 ≥ 0.35 mm — two surface sheets crossing within one cell, material intact). No zeroed lattice nodes. Refinement does not drive the count to 0 (2277 at h 0.25, 2197 at 0.15, 547 at 0.10 in probe 1; counts move but persist), so the boundary is honest: this is offset-surface TPMS at printable wall thickness with cell-width zipping. The control (`v8_probe3.rs`) meshes the plain solid bracket with the same mesher and reports ZERO defects of every class — non-manifold included — at h 0.25/0.15/0.10 (4.7/21.0/70.4 M verts; volume +0.006 %/+0.003 %/+0.006 % off the field auto-integral). The defects are therefore owned by the fill surface, not the mesher. The classification is the uniform path's; the adaptive export path below implements that named work — per-sheet vertices plus a head-of-weld chord registry — and measures 10 residual non-manifold edges on the same model at h = 0.25. Slicers take the solid as the union of the closed shell, which these crossings leave intact.

## Adaptive dual contouring (the browser export path)

1511698 verts, 3026110 triangles at h = 0.25, meshed in 1.02 s (uniform: 24.5 s; volume 23461.2 mm³, -0.098 % off the field reference). It refines to h only within the gradient certificate near the surface, which is what makes the GOAL 0.1 mm export budget reachable: the release CLI measured the 0.1 mm export — 9265984 verts, 18535066 triangles, mesh 8.5 s + 3MF write 0.08 s of export path (the same run's verification re-solve and defect scan add ~18 s) — against ≤ 15 s, versus the uniform mesher's 411 s native at the same spacing (probe 1). Its defect classes on the graded fill are measured (`adaptive_defect_census.rs`): 0 open edges, 10 non-manifold edges, 0 degenerate triangles, 4959 self-intersecting pairs at h = 0.25; the pre-separation census at the same spacing measured 997 / 269 / 343 / 34414, and at h = 0.1 the CLI now measures 8 / 229 / 4 / 7320 (pre-separation 8200 / 1213 / 2810 / 383834). The two former fan classes are closed by the stitches' one-vertex-per-sheet separation and chord registry: every multi-sheet leaf carries one vertex per surface sheet, so a leaf's fan pairs sheet-to-sheet instead of straddling a neighbouring sheet's tiles, and the weld registry — fan vertices sorted by (position hash, index), one head kept per distinct bitwise position, chords keyed by head pair — draws each chord between welded vertices exactly once, demoting any second quad on the same chord to triangulate along its own crossings chord. The structure the fix addresses is unchanged: 17598 of 3075633 leaves at h 0.25 carry two or more wall sheets in one cell (QEF residual > 0.3 cell), 233113 at h 0.1. What remains at h = 0.25: the 10 non-manifold edges are short spokes (10–220 µm) at lattice-aligned sheet-meeting vertices — the same welded lattice-corner class the double-gyroid fixture counts at 6 — with the fans' third corners ≤ 80 µm off the spoke axis; the 4959 self-intersecting pairs are interpenetrating wall tiles between neighbouring sheets' contours (normal-angle bins perp/mid/coplanar [166, 4778, 15]; the census's printed sample pairs sit 0.3–3.5 mm from the nearest residual edge endpoint). The uniform mesher on the same field reports 2277 non-manifold edges and none of the other classes. Refinement narrows these counts but does not erase them (the wall planes are self-similar at every level). The plain solid control on this path reports zero edges and geometry classes — the h 0.1 CLI scan measures it defect-free including self-intersections; `adaptive_plain_probe.rs` measured 51 crease-cell pairs before the separation.
