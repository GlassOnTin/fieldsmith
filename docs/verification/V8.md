## Method

The document `examples/bracket-filled.fsm` (name "Bracket", material `petg`) is built through the same `graded()` engine the Make lens calls, then handed to `fieldsmith-mesh::export::surface` at h = 0.25 mm. The volume reference is a field integral of the SAME graded field at h = 0.094 mm — the probe (`v8_probe.rs`, release) measured its fine-reference ladder at 23484.2 ± 12.6 mm³ there (0.05 %), so the field side of the comparison is fixed independently before the mesh is built. Defects come from `TriMesh::defects` (edge-map scan); self-intersections from the spatial-hash scan. The STL is written and read back; the 3MF is unpacked (central directory + raw-deflate inflate) and its model XML checked.

## Non-manifold edges of the graded fill

2277 edges with ≥ 3 incident triangles at h = 0.25. The classification probe (`v8_probe2.rs`) walks 26 directions per endpoint through the field: 43 of 3403 endpoints carry vanishing material (true pinch points of the fill surface, thickness → 0); every other endpoint keeps healthy interior either side (median span ≥ 1.2 mm, p10 ≥ 0.35 mm — two surface sheets crossing within one cell, material intact). No zeroed lattice nodes. Refinement does not drive the count to 0 (2277 at h 0.25, 2197 at 0.15, 547 at 0.10 in probe 1; counts move but persist), so the boundary is honest: this is offset-surface TPMS at printable wall thickness with cell-width zipping. The control (`v8_probe3.rs`) meshes the plain solid bracket with the same mesher and reports ZERO defects of every class — non-manifold included — at h 0.25/0.15/0.10 (4.7/21.0/70.4 M verts; volume +0.006 %/+0.003 %/+0.006 % off the field auto-integral). The defects are therefore owned by the fill surface, not the mesher. Named work: sub-cell sheet separation in the mesher, or a fill-side wall-crossing guard. Slicers take the solid as the union of the closed shell, which these crossings leave intact.

## Adaptive dual contouring (the browser export path)

1507430 verts, 3018006 triangles at h = 0.25, meshed in 0.94 s (uniform: 24.8 s; volume 23461.1 mm³, -0.098 % off the field reference). It refines to h only within the gradient certificate near the surface, which is what makes the GOAL 0.1 mm export budget reachable: the probe (`adaptive_probe.rs`) measured the budget line at 0.1 mm — mesh 7.0 s + 3MF 0.5 s = 7.5 s against ≤ 15 s, volume +0.038 %, versus the uniform mesher's 411 s native at the same spacing. Its defect classes on the graded fill are measured (`adaptive_defect_census.rs`): 997 open edges, 269 non-manifold edges, 343 degenerate triangles, 34414 self-intersecting pairs at h = 0.25, and 8200 / 1213 / 2810 / 383834 at h = 0.1. The uniform mesher on the same field reports none of those three classes, so they are adaptive-owned: 17598 of 3075633 leaves carry two or more wall sheets in one cell (QEF residual > 0.3 cell) — the same two-sheets-in-a-cell structure V8 classifies for the uniform mesher above — and the adaptive fan, one vertex per leaf, overlaps the sheets' tiles instead of abutting them; the open edges are slits along the wall-collapse lines and cross-level T-junctions of the flat wall patches, the degenerate triangles are fan corners fused by the exact weld at lattice corners lying on the wall planes. Refinement cannot close them (wall planes on the fill lattice are self-similar at every level, 17598 multi-sheet cells at h 0.25 → 233113 at h 0.1). Named work: sub-cell sheet separation in the adaptive stitch — per-sheet vertices — which is the same named work the uniform path's zipping class already carries. The plain solid control on this path reports zero edges and geometry classes, with 51 residual self-intersecting pairs from crease cells (`adaptive_plain_probe.rs`).
