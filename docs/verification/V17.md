## Replay (bit-exact)

`v17_rung_replay` drives the two-rung ladder straight through `analyse` — one `Instant` per rung, wall snapshotted before anything else reads it — and asserts the records recompute **bit-exactly** from the engine's own outputs: DOF, elements, element size from `Provenance`; tracked maxima from a hand-rolled floored envelope over cases; the change estimate equal to the two-solve difference. Nothing extra is clocked and nothing is invented: record construction gets a sentinel wall and must store it verbatim, so the record provably cannot re-clock or clamp.

## Cantilever ladder vs the known reference

The V1 beam through a three-rung ladder (aimed DOF 1000 → 30000 → 150000; the 30k→150k pair is the web schedule). The change estimate measures how much the solution MOVED between rungs — it is not a bound on how far the solution is from the truth, and the two regimes below say so from opposite directions.

Reference: δ = F·L³/(3·E·I) = 0.2000 mm; the envelope deflection is the tracked value the rung table uses (max displacement magnitude over the case); the tip probe is the Z deflection at the load face, what a user reads off.

**Pair 0→1 — the move can go AWAY from the theory.** At h≈7.8 mm the envelope sat at -0.042 % of beam theory; one refinement later (change +0.383 %) it sits at +0.341 %. The coarse rung's closeness was cancellation — roughly −0.35 % of discretisation under ~+0.31 % of model-form offset — not accuracy. A reader who took "within 0.1 % at the coarse rung" as convergence would be wrong, and a reader taking the change as the error's fate would get the wrong sign. At ship (referee, 2026-10-13) remaining offset / change = 0.889 (band [0.6, 1.2]; fp arithmetic, stable across machines).

**Pair 1→2 — converged onto bias.** The next rung moves the envelope +0.028 % — nearly nothing — while the offset to theory holds at +0.368 %. That offset is model form (the 3D clamp restrains Poisson contraction at the root; Euler–Bernoulli neglects shear — V1's discussion), which NO rung ladder can see or fix at any refinement. At ship remaining offset / change = 13.3 (band [8, 20]).

So the change estimates how much the solution MOVED, and both failure modes of reading it as a bound show up in this one beam: the small coarse offset was luck, and the tiny tail change understates the remaining offset by 13×. "Estimates, not bounds" is the only honest label.
