## Method

The document is compiled to its signed-distance field and integrated on a voxel grid (`fieldsmith_field::volume::volume_auto`, the same call the app and CLI use). Each voxel contributes a linear occupancy estimate from the field value and gradient at its centre, `clamp(0.5 − d/(|∇d|·h·‖n‖₁), 0, 1)`; blocks of 8³ voxels far from the surface are classified wholesale.
