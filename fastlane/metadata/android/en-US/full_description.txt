Fieldsmith is a parametric implicit CAD modeller, 3D quadratic (P2) finite-element solver, and lattice slicer built in Rust.

Workflow stages:
1. Shape: Construct parts from CSG primitives (box, cylinder, sphere, capsule, torus, extrude, revolve, loft), 2D constraint-solved sketches (Gauss-Newton solver with DOF and conflict tracking), built-in metric fasteners (bolts, screws, washers, hex/nyloc nuts, and snap-fits positioned by surface edge dimensions), and imported STL/OBJ/3MF/STEP meshes with fillet/chamfer blends and per-volume materials.
2. Setup: Assign surface regions, Dirichlet displacement supports, mechanical loads (force, pressure, bolt preload), and thermal boundary conditions (temperature, convection, heat flux). Evaluate fastener tightening torque windows (T_min, T_rec, T_max), Weibull brittle fracture statistics, polymer creep retention, and holdable axial/shear forces across 19 fastener and 24 substrate materials. Simulate dual-band (900 MHz and 2.4 GHz) drone antenna VNA sweeps (S11, S21, VSWR), 50 Ohm Smith chart impedance trajectories, 3D far-field radiation lobes, and carbon-fibre (CFRP) eddy-current loss with L-network matching.
3. Results: Solve 3D P2 linear elasticity, steady Fourier heat conduction, thermo-elastic coupling, and modal vibration. Inspect von Mises stress, safety factor, peel stress, deflection, and temperature fields with section planes and transient playback.
4. Fill: Grade triply periodic minimal surface (Gyroid, Schwarz-P, Diamond, Lidinoid, Neovius, Split-P) and strut lattices from stress fields or evolve boundaries via level-set topology optimization.
5. Make: Check minimum wall thickness and overhang angles, preview build layers, and export manifold STL or 3MF slice stacks.
6. Theory: Inspect the active governing equations, boundary operators, and numerical discretizations for the current model and export a Markdown or HTML math report.

Compute modes:
- On-device WebAssembly or embedded native arm64 Rust solver for offline mobile work.
- Optional remote compute connection over LAN, Tailscale, or WireGuard to offload large meshes and coupled multi-physics runs to a workstation running fieldsmith-mcp --serve.
