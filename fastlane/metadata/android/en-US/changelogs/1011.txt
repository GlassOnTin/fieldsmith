Initial F-Droid packaging for Fieldsmith v0.10.1.

- Six-stage mobile and desktop workbench: Shape, Setup, Results, Fill, Make, and Theory.
- 13 interactive tutorials and 5 starter .fsm models with searchable contents directory.
- Collapsible sidebars and slide-over drawers for mobile portrait 3D viewports.
- Embedded native arm64 HTTP/MCP compute bridge plus remote workstation compute offload.
